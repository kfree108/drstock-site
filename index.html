    Inventory doesn’t send you a bill.

    2. 02 · ContainerIt sits in a container, somewhere between the factory and the dock.
    3. 03 · WarehouseIt sits on a shelf in a warehouse, getting older.
    4. 04 · Over-orderedSome of it sits in units you over-ordered. Those age fastest.
    5. 05 · FeesSome of it leaves as fees you were charged incorrectly.
    6. 06 · SilenceNone of it arrives as an invoice. It just quietly stops being cash.

    FAQ

    Plain answers.

    How much will Dr. Stock recover for us?

    It depends on your catalog and your history, so the demo starts by checking your account: lost and damaged units, inbound shortages, returns that never came back, and fee overcharges. For reference, we’ve recovered $862,005 in FBA reimbursements for 35 brands (Feb 10–Sep 28, 2026). That’s past results, not a forecast for your account.

    Is Dr. Stock a person?

    No. Dr. Stock is a product: an AI agent powered by Opus 5.5. Opus 5.5 is Anthropic’s; ours is the system around it: the Full Circle playbooks, the daily checks and the approval levels you control.

    How does he decide when to reorder?

    He forecasts each SKU against its real lead time and how much that lead time varies, not a flat 30-day average. The output isn’t “reorder soon”; it’s the date the PO has to leave the supplier.

    Will he act without asking me?

    Only as much as you allow. You choose the approval level, from approving everything to letting him run on his own. Every day he checks the same six leaks; what changes is how much he does before you see it.

    What does he do about fee errors and missing units?

    He reconciles the fees you’re charged against the dimensions on file and requests a re-measure through Amazon’s own process when they don’t match. For inbound shipments, he reconciles shipped against received and files documented discrepancies inside Amazon’s windows.

    What does it cost?

    The first 30 days are free, with no contract. Orbit is included free. If you’re on Dr. PPC, Dr. Stock is already included. Reimbursement recovery is 10% of what we get back, and nothing if we recover nothing. We’ll cover the rest in the 20-minute demo.

    How do reimbursements work?

    We find the places Amazon owes you: units lost or damaged in Amazon’s fulfillment network, inbound shipments received short, returns that never came back or came back damaged, and fee overcharges from wrong dimensions. Every claim goes through Amazon’s own case process, inside Amazon’s windows, with the evidence attached, and we follow it until it’s paid or closed.

    You keep 90%. We take 10% of what we get back, and nothing if we recover nothing.

    Is the freight actually free?

    It’s free until it lands. You don’t pay the freight bill until your products are delivered in the US, instead of at or before departure. The freight is still billed; the point is that the cash stays in your business for the whole trip. Terms and eligibility are confirmed on the call.

    Is this the right door for us?

    If inventory is where the money leaks, yes. If your stock is healthy and the leak is in the ad account, start with Dr. PPC. If the problem is operational across catalogue, listings and supply chain, Full Circle Agency is the better fit. See the wrong-door notes below.
